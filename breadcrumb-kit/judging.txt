CORE SCRIPT — ADAPT TO WHAT IS BUILT
"Finding the building is often easy. Finding the right room inside it can mean calling the host at every turn.

Breadcrumb lets the host record that walk once, then gives visitors a guide that responds to what their camera sees.

Here is our recording. Gemini turns it into landmark checkpoints, and the host reviews the directions before sharing the route.

Now this is a different phone following it. It recognizes the landmark and gives the next instruction. When the view is unclear, it asks for a better look instead of inventing a turn. When the destination sign appears, it confirms arrival.

The visitor can also hear the guide in their chosen language, using ElevenLabs. [Demonstrate the implemented language switch.]

The technical core combines visual evidence with progress along an approved route. Recognizing a place alone does not establish which way you face, so uncertain orientation pauses directional guidance.

We built this for event hosts and visitors in unfamiliar buildings. One recorded walk becomes reusable directions people can actually check against their surroundings."

Do not say 'live camera' if presenting replay. Use 'this is a recorded second-phone walk being processed by our engine' for actual replay processing. For a finished video say 'this is a recording of the app working'. Use cached voice honestly; caching approved instructions is a sensible engineering choice.

Q&A
Why not a video? A video requires the visitor to find the matching moment. Our implemented advantage must be matching their current view and responding to uncertainty. If matching fails, acknowledge the limitation.
Is this real AR? We overlay guidance on the camera. Floor/world-anchored AR is a future extension unless actually built.
Can it reroute anywhere? No. The initial prototype follows an approved recorded path. Unknown views trigger clarification. A connected verified route graph would be needed for arbitrary rerouting.
Does it work for blind people? We offer spoken guidance and readable captions; we have not validated this as a blind mobility or obstacle-avoidance aid.
What if the hallway looks similar? Use sign text, multiple reference angles, temporal evidence and route context; fall back to uncertainty when ambiguous.
What did AI code? Accurately disclose the tools and substantial generated components; explain your architecture, integration and testing contributions. Follow event policy once confirmed.
How accurate is it? Give only measured observations with the number of trials. Distinguish route completion, false matches and response latency.
What does Tiger Data add? Only claim the quality-feedback loop if it works. Generic persistence alone is not our competitive argument.
